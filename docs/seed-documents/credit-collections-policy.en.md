# Northstar Digital — Credit & Collections Policy

**Version:** 2.1 · **Effective date:** 15 January 2026 · **Owner:** Finance (Mark Ellis) · **Approved by:** Managing Director (Anna Petrova)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines how Northstar Digital SIA invoices clients, reminds them about payment, applies late-payment
charges and escalates overdue receivables. It applies to every client invoice, whether it was created in PayFlow Guard
or imported from another tool. Where a client contract says something different, the contract wins for that client.

## 2. Payment terms

- Standard payment terms are **Net 14** (14 calendar days from the invoice date).
- Key accounts may receive up to **Net 45** with written approval of the Managing Director. Current exceptions:
  Orbit Fintech (Net 45), Helios Media Group (Net 30, per MSA), Studio Alba (Net 30), Kraft & Hale (Net 30), Nordlicht Apps (Net 30).
- Invoices are issued on milestone acceptance or on the first business day of the month for retainers.
- Clients who require purchase-order numbers must have the PO number on the invoice before it is sent.

## 3. Reminder cadence

PayFlow Guard sends reminders automatically according to the default policy "Standard agency cadence":

| When (relative to the due date) | Tone | Who else is involved |
|---|---|---|
| 3 days before the due date | friendly | — |
| On the due date | friendly | — |
| 4 days after | friendly | — |
| 9 days after | neutral | — |
| 14 days after | neutral, with a late-fee notice where the contract allows it | — |
| 21 days after | neutral (firm for systematically late clients) | the account manager calls the client |
| 30 days after | firm, with a late-fee notice | the Managing Director is notified |
| 45 days after | final notice | the Managing Director decides on suspension of work |

- No more than **8 automatic reminders** per invoice; at least **3 days** between reminders.
- Reminders are sent on business days between 09:00 and 17:00 Riga time.
- After the final notice, if there is no payment or agreed payment plan within 7 days, work may be suspended in accordance with
  the client's contract. Suspension always requires the Managing Director's approval.

**When should we remind a particular client?** Follow the automatic schedule above. For clients classified as *systematically late*
(at least half of their invoices paid late and on average 10 or more days late) the account manager should also call the client at
+9 days instead of waiting for +21.

## 4. Tone of communication

- Up to 7 days overdue: friendly. 8–21 days: neutral. 22–44 days: firm. 45 days or more: final.
- A final notice is never sent before an invoice is 30 days overdue unless the step is configured explicitly.
- Systematically late clients receive a tone one step firmer than the schedule.
- Key accounts with a tone ceiling (currently Orbit Fintech) never receive more than a neutral tone; escalation happens by phone.
- Templates and wording are described in the "Reminder Templates & Tone Guide".

## 5. Disputes

- If a client disputes an invoice, mark the invoice as **disputed** in PayFlow Guard. Reminders stop automatically.
- The account manager must resolve the dispute within **10 business days** and document the outcome on the invoice.
- The undisputed part of an invoice remains payable.

## 6. Late-payment charges

- Late-payment charges are applied **only if the client's contract provides for them**.
- Default contract clause (used when a client has no specific terms): **1% per month** of the overdue amount, calculated daily,
  after a **7-day grace period**, capped at **10%** of the invoice amount.
- Client-specific terms override the default (for example, the Helios Media Group MSA and the Baltic Logistics contract).
- Waivers: the finance manager may waive charges up to $500 / €500 per invoice; larger waivers need the Managing Director.
- Charges are added to the invoice as a separate "late payment charge" line, never silently.

## 7. Credit holds and payment plans

- A client with more than **$15,000 (or €15,000)** overdue by more than 30 days is placed on credit hold: new work requires a 50% prepayment.
- Payment plans of up to **3 instalments** may be agreed by the finance manager and must be confirmed in writing.

## 8. Escalation and write-off

| Days overdue | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | phone call to the client's accounts-payable contact | account manager |
| 30 | escalation to the client's finance lead; MD informed | finance manager |
| 45 | final notice; decision on suspension | Managing Director |
| 90 | external collection agency (only with MD approval) | Managing Director |
| 180 | write-off as uncollectible (MD approval) | finance manager |

## 9. Records

All reminders, payments, disputes and write-offs are recorded in PayFlow Guard. Do not delete issued invoices — void them with a reason.
