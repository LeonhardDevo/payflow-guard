# Master Services Agreement — Helios Media Group

**Agreement No.:** NSD-MSA-2025-014 · **Date:** 1 March 2025 · **Effective date:** 1 March 2025

**Parties:** Northstar Digital SIA, Brīvības iela 68, Rīga, LV-1011, Latvia ("Northstar", the Contractor) and
Helios Media Group Inc., 410 Hudson Street, New York, NY 10014, USA ("Helios", the Client).

## 1. Subject

Northstar provides design and web development services described in Statements of Work ("SOWs") signed under this Agreement,
including the corporate website redesign (SOW-2025-03) and a monthly support retainer (SOW-2025-07).

## 2. Fees and invoicing

2.1. Project work is invoiced per milestone after written acceptance by Helios's product owner. Acceptance is deemed given if
Helios raises no written objection within 5 business days of delivery.
2.2. The support retainer is invoiced monthly in advance at USD 4,500 per month.
2.3. All amounts are in US dollars and exclude taxes, which are added where applicable.

## 3. Payment terms

3.1. Invoices are payable within **thirty (30) days** of the invoice date ("Net 30").
3.2. Payment is made by bank transfer to the account stated on the invoice or by card through the payment link provided by Northstar.
3.3. Helios shall quote the invoice number in the payment reference.

## 4. Late payment

4.1. Any amount not paid by the due date bears a **late-payment charge of 1.5% per month** on the overdue amount, **calculated daily**,
starting after a **grace period of ten (10) days** after the due date.
4.2. The total late-payment charge for an invoice shall not exceed **fifteen percent (15%)** of that invoice's amount.
4.3. Northstar may add accrued late-payment charges to a subsequent invoice or as a separate line on the overdue invoice.

## 5. Disputes

5.1. Helios shall notify Northstar in writing of any disputed invoice within **seven (7) days** of receipt, stating the reasons in reasonable detail.
5.2. The undisputed part of an invoice remains payable on its due date.
5.3. The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve disputes within ten (10) business days.

## 6. Suspension

6.1. If any undisputed invoice remains unpaid more than **thirty (30) days** after its due date, Northstar may suspend the services after giving
**five (5) business days'** written notice, without liability for resulting delays.
6.2. Services resume within three (3) business days after all overdue amounts are paid.

## 7. Bank details

7.1. Each party shall notify the other of any change of its bank details **only by a letter signed by an authorised signatory**, and the change
shall be **confirmed by a telephone call to a contact number previously agreed** between the parties. Payment instructions received by email alone
shall not be acted upon.

## 8. Term and termination

8.1. This Agreement remains in force until terminated by either party with sixty (60) days' written notice.
8.2. Fees for services performed until termination remain payable.
