## 1. Subject

1.1. The Contractor provides support and development of the integration between the Customer's warehouse system and its partner portal
(the "Services"); the Customer accepts and pays for the Services.
1.2. The monthly scope is agreed by email; hours actually worked are recorded in a monthly report.

## 2. Fees and payment

2.1. The Contractor's rate is **EUR 110 per hour**, excluding VAT.
2.2. The Contractor invoices monthly, no later than the 5th day of the following month, based on the hours report.
2.3. The Customer pays each invoice within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of the invoice date.
2.4. Payment is made by bank transfer to the Contractor's account stated on the invoice or through the Contractor's payment link,
quoting the invoice number.

## 3. Liability

3.1. For late payment the Customer pays the Contractor a **penalty of 0.1% (one tenth of one percent) of the unpaid amount for each calendar
day of delay**, but **not more than 10% (ten percent) of the amount of the respective invoice**.
3.2. The penalty accrues from the day following the last day of the payment term, without any grace period.
3.3. Payment of the penalty does not release the Customer from the obligation to pay the invoice.
3.4. If payment is more than **thirty (30) days** late, the Contractor may suspend the Services by giving at least **three (3) business days'**
written notice.

## 4. Acceptance and claims

4.1. The Customer submits any claim regarding an invoice or hours report within **five (5) business days** of receiving the invoice.
4.2. If no claim is submitted within this period, the Services are deemed accepted.

## 5. Bank details

5.1. A party notifies the other of a change of its bank details **by a letter signed by its director**. The change takes effect only after
**confirmation by telephone** using the number in section 8. Notifications received by email only are not grounds for changing bank details.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1. The parties shall not disclose information received under this Agreement to third parties, except as required by law.

## 7. Term

7.1. The Agreement is valid until 31 December 2026 and is automatically extended for each following year unless either party gives notice
of termination 30 days before expiry.
