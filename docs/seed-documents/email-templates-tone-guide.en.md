# Reminder Templates & Tone Guide

**Version:** 1.4 · **Effective date:** 1 April 2026 · **Owner:** Finance

This guide explains how Northstar Digital writes payment reminders. PayFlow Guard picks the tone automatically; people editing a reminder
before sending it manually must keep to the same rules.

## 1. The tone ladder

| Tone | Typical timing | Goal | Must include | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Friendly | before the due date up to 7 days late | a helpful nudge — most late payments are simple oversights | invoice number, amount, due date, payment link | the word "overdue" before the due date, any pressure |
| Neutral | 8–21 days late | ask for a payment date | the number of days overdue, a question ("when can we expect payment?"), offer to fix issues | sarcasm, "as previously mentioned" chains |
| Firm | 22–44 days late (earlier for systematically late clients) | ask for payment within a specific period | a concrete deadline (5 business days), late-fee notice if the contract allows it, request for remittance details | threats, capital letters, exclamation marks |
| Final | 45+ days late, never before 30 days | last written notice before suspension | the consequence exactly as stated in the contract (e.g. suspension of work), 7-day deadline, an offer to agree a payment plan | legal threats not provided for in the contract, debt-collector language |

## 2. Writing rules

1. Lead with the facts: invoice number, amount due, due date.
2. One request per email. Make the payment link the most visible element.
3. Assume good faith in friendly and neutral reminders. Do not blame.
4. Mention late-payment charges only if the client's contract provides for them, and quote the accrued amount from PayFlow Guard.
5. Never attach new bank details to a reminder. Our receiving bank details are printed on the invoice and never change by email.
6. Sign with a real person's name (the account manager or finance manager).
7. Russian-language clients receive Russian emails: address them with «вы», avoid bureaucratic phrases («доводим до вашего сведения»).

## 3. Subject lines

- Friendly: "Friendly reminder: invoice NS-2026-0150 is due in 3 days".
- Neutral: "Invoice NS-2026-0145 is 9 days overdue".
- Firm: "Action required: invoice NS-2026-0131 — $3,000.00 overdue".
- Final: "Final notice: invoice NS-2026-0118 ($12,400.00)".

## 4. When to call instead of writing

- The client is classified as systematically late and the invoice is 9+ days overdue.
- Two reminders in a row were not answered.
- The invoice amount exceeds $10,000 / €10,000.
- The client asked for a payment plan or disputes the invoice — a call followed by a written summary is faster.

## 5. Examples of good sentences

- "If the payment is already on its way, thank you — please ignore this message."
- "Could you let us know when we can expect payment?"
- "If there is an issue with the invoice, reply to this email and we'll sort it out."
- "Unless we receive payment or an agreed payment plan within 7 days, we will have to pause ongoing work in accordance with our agreement."

## 6. Examples to avoid

- "This is your LAST chance!!!"
- "We will take legal action immediately." (unless the contract and the Managing Director say so)
- "Please pay to our new account below." (we never change bank details by email)
