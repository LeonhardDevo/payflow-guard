This Cookie Policy explains which cookies and similar technologies {{product.name}} at {{product.url}} uses. It is provided by {{company.name}}.

## 1. Short version

We use only cookies that are strictly necessary to provide the Service you request. We do not use analytics, advertising, social media or other tracking cookies, and we load no third-party scripts, fonts or images. Because strictly necessary cookies do not require consent under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive and comparable laws, we show a short information notice instead of a consent banner.

## 2. Cookies we use

| Name                                       | Purpose                                                                                             | Duration                                  | Type                                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Session cookie (name ends with `_session`) | keeps you signed in and links requests to your session; contents are encrypted                      | until you sign out or the session expires | first-party, strictly necessary, HttpOnly         |
| `XSRF-TOKEN`                               | protects forms and requests against cross-site request forgery                                      | same as the session                       | first-party, strictly necessary                   |
| `locale`                                   | remembers the interface language you chose (English or Russian)                                     | 1 year                                    | first-party, strictly necessary (user preference) |
| `appearance`                               | remembers the light, dark or system theme you chose and prevents a flash of the wrong theme on load | 1 year                                    | first-party, strictly necessary (user preference) |
| `sidebar_state`                            | remembers whether the application sidebar is expanded or collapsed                                  | 1 year                                    | first-party, strictly necessary (user preference) |
| Cookie-notice dismissal                    | remembers that you closed the cookie notice, so it is not shown again                               | 1 year                                    | first-party, strictly necessary                   |

The Service may also store the same theme and language preferences in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is used only to render the interface as you configured it.

## 3. What we do not do

- No analytics or performance measurement cookies.
- No advertising, retargeting or cross-site tracking.
- No third-party content (CDNs, web fonts, embedded videos, social buttons) that could set cookies.

Payment pages of our payment provider open on the provider's own domain and are governed by its cookie policy.

## 4. Future changes

If we ever wanted to use cookies that are not strictly necessary (for example analytics), we would first update this Policy and ask for your prior, opt-in consent; such cookies would not be set until you agree, and you could withdraw consent at any time.

## 5. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, you will not be able to sign in, and your language and theme preferences will not be remembered.
