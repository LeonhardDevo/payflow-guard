## 1. Unlawful use and rights of others

You must not use the Service to:

- violate any applicable law or regulation, including data protection, consumer protection, anti-spam, export control and sanctions laws;

- upload content that is illegal, including child sexual abuse material, content promoting terrorism or violence, or content that incites hatred;
- defraud others, impersonate any person or organisation, or misrepresent your affiliation.

## 2. Harassment and abuse of communication features

You must not use e-mail, reminders, notifications, the Telegram bot or any other messaging feature of the Service to:

- send unsolicited bulk or commercial messages (spam), or messages to recipients who have opted out;
- harass, threaten, intimidate or abuse any person;
- send messages at a frequency or in a manner that a reasonable recipient would consider abusive;
- disguise the origin of messages or remove the opt-out link from automated messages.

## 3. Sensitive data

Do not upload special categories of personal data (such as health, biometric, genetic data, data about racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade union membership, sex life or sexual orientation), data about criminal convictions, or payment card numbers, unless the Service explicitly supports that use and you have a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.

## 4. Security and integrity

You must not:

- probe, scan or test the vulnerability of the Service without our written permission, except under the responsible disclosure process described at {{product.url}}/.well-known/security.txt;
- bypass authentication, access controls, usage limits or workspace isolation;
- access or attempt to access another customer's data;
- introduce malware, or interfere with or disrupt the Service, its infrastructure or other users;
- use automated means to access the Service other than our documented interfaces, or at a rate that degrades the Service.

## 5. AI Features

You must not use AI Features to:

- generate content that is unlawful, deceptive or harmful, or to impersonate real persons;
- make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects for individuals without meaningful human review;
- attempt to extract other customers' data, system instructions or credentials from the models (prompt injection);
- circumvent safety measures of the Service or of AI providers.

We may investigate suspected violations and, depending on severity, warn you, remove or disable content, suspend members, workspaces or features, or terminate the Terms as described there.
