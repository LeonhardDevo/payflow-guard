This is an English translation provided for information. The legally relevant text is the Russian version, which users registering in the Russian locale accept under Article 9 of Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data".

By registering with {{product.name}} at {{product.url}} and ticking the consent box, I, the data subject, freely, of my own will and in my own interest, give specific, informed, conscious and unambiguous consent to the processing of my personal data on the following terms.

## 1. Operator

{{company.name}}, registration number {{company.registration}}, address: {{company.address}}, e-mail for personal data requests: {{company.privacy_email}}.

## 2. Purposes of processing

- registering and maintaining my account, providing access to the Service and workspaces;
- performing the contract concluded with me (Terms of Service), including support;
- securing the Service, authentication, preventing fraud and abuse, keeping an audit log;
- sending service notifications by e-mail and, if I link it, via Telegram;
- managing subscriptions, payments and compliance with tax and accounting law;
- recording my acceptance of the Service's legal documents.

## 3. Personal data concerned

surname and first name (as entered by me); e-mail address; password hash; interface language and appearance settings; two-factor authentication and passkey data; IP address, browser and device information (user agent), date and time of sign-ins and actions; roles in workspaces; if Telegram is linked, Telegram user and chat identifiers, username and language code; questions to the assistant and answers; payment information other than card data; records of acceptance of legal documents (version, date, time, IP address, user agent, language).

## 4. Actions and methods of processing

Collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access) to the persons listed in section 5, depersonalisation, blocking, deletion and destruction. Processing is carried out by mixed means, with automation and transmission over information and telecommunication networks.

## 5. Recipients

Sub-processors acting on the Operator's instruction (hosting, e-mail delivery, payment providers and other persons listed at {{product.url}}/legal/subprocessors), and owners and administrators of workspaces I am a member of, within their roles. Cross-border transfer takes place only in compliance with Article 12 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ; recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification and retrieval of personal data of Russian citizens are carried out using databases located in the Russian Federation.

## 6. Duration and withdrawal

This consent is valid from the moment it is given until my account is deleted or the consent is withdrawn. I may withdraw consent by writing to {{company.privacy_email}} or by deleting my account in Settings → Privacy. Upon withdrawal, the Operator stops processing and destroys the personal data within 30 days of receipt, unless otherwise provided by law; the Operator may continue processing without consent where grounds under paragraphs 2–11 of Article 6(1) of Federal Law No. 152-FZ apply. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

## 7. Confirmation

I confirm that I have read the Privacy Policy ({{product.url}}/legal/privacy), that my rights under Articles 14–17 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ and the consequences of withdrawing consent have been explained to me, and that I am at least 18 years old.
