## How to read this list

- **Always used** sub-processors are needed to run the Service for every customer.
- **Used only when enabled** sub-processors receive data only after a workspace owner turns on the related feature (for example linking Telegram or allowing external AI providers). If a workspace uses only built-in or local AI models, no AI provider receives its content.
- Payment providers act as independent controllers for payment processing and fraud prevention and are listed for transparency.
