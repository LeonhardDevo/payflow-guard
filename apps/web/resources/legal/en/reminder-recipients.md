You are reading this notice because a business sent you an invoice, a payment reminder or a payment receipt through {{product.name}}. This page explains who processes your data, why, for how long, and what choices you have.

## Who is responsible

The business that issued the invoice (the "sender") is the controller of your data. It decides which invoices are sent, to whom and when. {{product.name}}, provided by {{company.name}}, processes the data only on the sender's behalf and under a data processing agreement. The sender's privacy contact is printed in the footer of every e-mail and on every invoice page.

## What is processed

- Your name and the business e-mail address used for invoices, and any copy recipients the sender added.
- The invoice data: numbers, amounts, due dates, line items and payment status.
- Whether and when the invoice page was opened, if the sender has view tracking switched on.
- The payment history of your organisation with the sender (paid on time or late, by how many days). The sender uses it to plan the timing and tone of reminders and to estimate when a payment is likely to arrive.

No profiling with legal or similarly significant effect takes place. Risk estimates are decision support for the sender's team only and are never shown to you or used to make automated decisions about you.

## Why and on what basis

The data is processed to issue invoices, collect payment for services that were delivered and keep accounting records. The legal basis is the performance of the contract between your organisation and the sender and the sender's legitimate interest in being paid on time.

## How long

Invoices and payments are financial records and are kept for the statutory period that applies to the sender, usually up to ten years. The text of reminder e-mails is deleted after one year; the date, tone and status of each reminder are kept with the invoice. Invoice PDF files are cached for up to seven days and generated again when needed.

## Your choices

- **Already paid?** Use the link "Already paid or want to stop automated reminders" in any e-mail. You can tell the sender that the invoice is paid and add a payment reference. Automated reminders for that invoice pause for seven days while the sender checks the payment.
- **Stop automated reminders.** On the same page you can stop all automated reminders from this sender. The sender is notified immediately. Invoices and individual messages written by a person may still reach you, because the underlying debt does not disappear.
- **One-click unsubscribe.** E-mail clients that support the standard unsubscribe button stop automated reminders in one click.

A person at the sender can always be contacted by replying to any e-mail.

## Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest and to complain to a supervisory authority. Please contact the sender first using the privacy contact in the e-mail footer. If you cannot reach the sender, you can write to {{company.privacy_email}} and we will forward your request.
